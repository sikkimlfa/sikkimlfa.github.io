Statutory Overview

The Directorate of Local Fund Audit functions under the administrative control of the Finance Department, Government of Sikkim. It serves as the apex statutory audit authority tasked with examining the financial accounts, statutory compliance, and fund utilization across local bodies, Panchayati Raj Institutions (PRIs), Urban Local Bodies (ULBs), and designated state entities.

Core Mandates & Functions

  • Statutory Financial Audits: Conducting continuous and periodic inspection of accounts maintained by local funds and assigned authorities in accordance with state financial codes.
  • Panchayati Raj & ULB Reviews: Scrutinizing expenditure statements, fund sanctions, and revenue collections of Gram Panchayats, Zilla Panchayats, and Municipal bodies.
  • Grant Utilization Checks: Verifying Utilization Certificates (UCs) for state and central sponsored schemes to ensure adherence to statutory norms.
  • Audit Reports & Recommendations: Issuing formal audit inspection notes, tracking action taken reports (ATRs), and advising departments on sound financial management.

Regulatory Framework

All audits and scrutiny processes are governed under:

  • The Sikkim Local Fund Audit Act and corresponding State Financial Rules.
  • Treasury rules and circulars issued by the Finance Department, Government of Sikkim.
  • Applicable audit standards and accounting frameworks established for public fund regulation.

# Directorate of Local Fund Audit, Government of Sikkim

## Statutory Audit & Financial Governance

---

### **About Us**

Operating under the statutory mandate of **The Sikkim Local Fund Audit Act, 2012 (Act No. 22 of 2012)** and the **Sikkim Local Fund Audit Rules, 2014**, the **Directorate of Local Fund Audit (DLFA)** functions as an independent financial guardian and oversight body under the Finance, Revenue and Expenditure Department, Government of Sikkim.

Our core mandate is to uphold public financial propriety, ensure strict statutory compliance, and safeguard public funds across all tiers of local governance within the State.

---

### **Jurisdiction & Scope of Audit**

The Directorate exercises post-audit and financial verification over all Local Authorities in Sikkim, covering both rural and urban administration:

* **Panchayati Raj Institutions (PRIs):** Zilla Panchayats and Gram Panchayats constituted under the *Sikkim Panchayat Act, 1993*.
* **Urban Local Bodies (ULBs):** Municipal Corporations, Municipal Councils, and Nagar Panchayats established under the *Sikkim Municipalities Act, 2007*.
* **Local Funds:** Funds administered by local authorities or other designated bodies entrusted with public control and management.

---

### **Key Responsibilities & Functions**

1. **Transaction & Compliance Audit:** Conducting rigorous post-audits of accounts, receipts, expenditures, stores, and stock balances to verify legality and propriety.
2. **Detection of Financial Irregularities:** Identifying unauthorized procurements, lack of valid vouchers, unauthenticated expenditures, and procedural rule violations (e.g., deviations from the Sikkim Financial Rules, 1979).
3. **Surcharge and Disallowance:** Exercising statutory authority to disallow illegal or irregular expenditure, levy surcharges, and fix accountability against responsible disbursing officers and executive authorities.
4. **Objection Statements & Rectification:** Issuing formal audit observations, Objection Statements (Form-II), and requiring Executive Authorities to submit comprehensive Rectification Reports accompanied by authenticated vouchers or ex-post facto relaxation orders within the statutory timeframe (three months).
5. **Legislative Accountability:** Compiling and submitting the Annual Consolidated Audit Report to the State Government for presentation before the Sikkim Legislative Assembly, ensuring complete transparency and compliance with Finance Commission requirements.

---

### **Our Professional Standards**

In strict alignment with statutory obligations, our communication and reporting framework is **objective, fact-based, and dispassionate**. Every audit observation is backed by explicit rule citations, financial limits, and legal provisions—maintaining the authoritative yet polite posture required to enforce administrative accountability, transparency, and fiscal discipline across the grassroots governance structure of Sikkim.

Comprehensive Guide to RTI Proactive Disclosure and Internal Audit Functions in Sikkim#

Transparency and accountability are the bedrock of effective financial management in government. Under Section 4(1)(b) of the Right to Information (RTI) Act, 2005, public authorities are mandated to proactively disclose key information regarding their organization, functions, duties, and operational procedures. This proactive disclosure minimizes the necessity for individual citizens to file formal requests while fostering public trust.

This article details the institutional framework, RTI proactive disclosures, and core objectives of both the Sikkim Local Fund Audit (SLFA) and the Internal Audit (IA) Division under the Finance Department, Government of Sikkim.

Part 1: Proactive Disclosure under Section 4(1)(b) of the RTI Act, 2005#

1. Particulars of Organization, Functions, and Duties [Section 4(1)(b)(i)]#

  • Organization: Sikkim Local Fund Audit (SLFA) & Internal Audit Division, Finance Department, Government of Sikkim.
  • Address: 4th Floor, Block B, Tashiling Secretariat, Gangtok, Sikkim.
  • Head of Organization: Mr. Karma Loda Tshering, Additional Director.
  • Vision: To serve as a premier institution in local body and public finance auditing, providing high-quality, independent, and objective services to ensure financial transparency and accountability across local governance structures in Sikkim.
  • Mission: To deliver timely, comprehensive, and effective audit services for local bodies and state government institutions, ensuring that public resources are utilized efficiently, transparently, and strictly for their sanctioned purposes.

2. Powers and Duties of Officers and Employees [Section 4(1)(b)(ii)]#

  • Statutory Basis: Audit functions are carried out under the Sikkim Local Fund Audit Act, 2012 and Sikkim Local Fund Audit Rules, 2014.
  • Auditor Authority: Auditors appointed under the Act are empowered to examine accounts, call for requisitions, issue audit queries, and evaluate statements of accounts for local authorities listed under the Schedule of the Act.
  • Internal Audit Role: Internal Auditors assess the operational efficacy of internal financial controls within state departments, examining financial statements, grant-in-aid disbursements, and voucher reconciliation.

3. Decision-Making Process and Supervision [Section 4(1)(b)(iii)]#

  • Process Flow: Financial and administrative decisions are processed on official files through structured noting, scrutiny, and authorization by designated supervisory authorities.
  • Channels of Accountability: Auditors report to Team Leaders/Sr. Accounts Officers, who escalate key findings through Joint Directors/Additional Directors to the Director of Local Fund Audit and the Finance Department.

4. Norms and Standards for Discharge of Functions [Section 4(1)(b)(iv)]#

  • Audit Execution: Audits are scheduled and executed according to an officially approved Annual Audit Plan.
  • Timelines: Issuance of audit requisitions, completion of field audits, drafting of audit observations, and finalization of Annual Consolidated Reports adhere strictly to timelines set under the Sikkim LFA Rules.

5. Rules, Regulations, Instructions, and Manuals Held [Section 4(1)(b)(v)]#

  • Legislative Framework:
    • The Sikkim Local Fund Audit Act, 2012 (Act 22 of 2012)
    • The Sikkim Local Fund Audit Rules, 2012 / 2014
    • Central Secretariat Manual of Office Procedure (CSMOP)
    • Financial Rules and Treasury Codes of the Government of Sikkim

6. Documents and Record Categories [Section 4(1)(b)(vi)]#

  • Audit Files & Reports: Audit reports sent under Section 13 of the Act, including special audits, test audits, and annual consolidated reports.
  • Financial Statements: Form-III (Statement of Grants), Form-IV (Statement of Loans), and annual statements of receipts and payments.
  • RTI Records: Registers of RTI applications, first appeals, proactive disclosures, and training documentation.

7. RTI Implementation & Contact Directory [Section 4(1)(b)(xvi)]#

  • Nodal Officer (RTI): Sh. Devendra Rai, Joint Director
  • State Public Information Officer (SPIO): Mr. Palden Thakarpa, Sr. Accounts Officer
  • Assistant State Public Information Officer (ASPIO): Mr. Phuchung Bhutia, Accounts Officer
  • Contact Email: sikkimlfa@gmail.com
  • Online RTI Portal: rtionline.sikkim.gov.in

Part 2: Core Objectives of the Internal Audit (IA) Division#

While the Sikkim Local Fund Audit Directorate primarily focuses on local authorities (Zilla Panchayats, Gram Panchayats, and Municipalities), the Internal Audit (IA) Division performs vital internal financial oversight across state government departments and Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs).

Key Objectives:#

  1. Evaluation of Internal Control Systems

    • Reviewing financial, personnel, and operational administrative mechanisms across government departments to ensure that adequate internal control frameworks are established and actively enforced.

  2. Compliance and Statutory Verification

    • Verifying whether financial transactions, procurement processes, and administrative actions conform strictly to state financial rules, budget authorizations, and legislative mandates.

  3. Grants-in-Aid and Financial Assistance Oversight

    • Ensuring that loans, advances, and grants-in-aid are correctly disbursed, properly accounted for, utilized for sanctioned purposes, and that any overpayments or improper transfers are promptly identified and recovered.

  4. Voucher and Payment Verification

    • Conducting detailed audits of lists of payments accompanied by paid vouchers submitted by Drawing and Disbursing Officers (DDOs) to verify authenticity and accuracy.

  5. Reconciliation of Bank Scrolls and Accounts

    • Verifying that receipt and payment scrolls, bank challans, and paid cheques are received from authorized bank branches within stipulated timeframes and reconciled accurately against official treasury records.

  6. System Enhancement and Risk Mitigation

    • Recommending systemic updates and process improvements to minimize financial leakages, strengthen internal checks, and optimize resource allocation.

Conclusion#

The active implementation of proactive disclosures under the RTI Act, paired with a robust Internal Audit framework, forms a powerful governance mechanism. By making financial operations transparent and subjecting departmental processes to internal evaluation, the Finance Department of the Government of Sikkim reinforces its commitment to transparent, accountable, and citizen-centric governance.

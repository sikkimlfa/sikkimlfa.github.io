Understanding the Sikkim Zilla Panchayat (Financial) Rules: A Comprehensive Guide

August 4, 2026 · 15 min · Directorate of Local Fund Audit

The Sikkim Zilla Panchayat (Financial) Rules, 2001—along with its subsequent amendment in 2004—forms the primary framework governing financial operations, budget preparation, fund management, and expenditure approvals for Zilla Panchayats across Sikkim.

Below is a detailed overview and verbatim compilation of the official notifications, rules, and schedules published by the Rural Development Department (now Rural Management & Development Department), Government of Sikkim.

Government Notification (2001)#

GOVERNMENT OF SIKKIM RURAL DEVELOPMENT DEPARTMENT

No. 35(35)97-98/6/RDD/P. Dated: 24.8.2001.

NOTIFICATION#

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 130 of the Sikkim Panchayat Act, 1993 (6 of 1993), the State Government hereby makes the following rules, namely:-[cite: 1]

1. Short title and commencement. (1) These rules may be called the Sikkim Zilla Panchayat (Financial) Rules, 2001[cite: 1]. (2) They shall come into force at once[cite: 1].

2. Definitions (1) In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires:-[cite: 1]

  • “Act” means the Sikkim Panchayat Act, 1993[cite: 1].
  • “Secretary” means the Secretary to the Government in the Rural Development Department or any other officer designated as such by the State Government[cite: 1].
  • “Schedule” means the Schedules appended to these rules[cite: 1].

(2) Words defined in the Sikkim Panchayat Act, 1993 but not defined in these rules shall have the same meanings as respectively assigned to them in the Sikkim Panchayat Act, 1993[cite: 1].

3. Establishment of Zilla Panchayat Fund. (1) The Zilla Panchayat fund established under sub-section (1) of section 76 of the Act shall be held in the Savings Bank Account with the State Bank of Sikkim or any other Nationalised Bank having its branch at the respective District Headquarter and also at Gangtok[cite: 1]. (2) All monies received by the Zilla Panchayat from various sources as indicated at clause (a) to (g) of sub-section (1) of section 76 of the Act shall be deposited by the Sachiva in the Zilla Panchayat Fund Account on the same day and the entry made in the Cash Book as required under rule 4 of these rules[cite: 1].

  • Provided that where it is not possible to deposit the cash or cheque in the Zilla Panchayat Fund Account, the Sachiva will keep the same under his safe custody and deposit the sums on the next working day in the bank where such account is maintained[cite: 1].
  • Provided further that no sums in cash or cheque, shall be kept without depositing the same in the account for more than three months without obtaining the approval of the Secretary, Rural Development Department[cite: 1].

(3) The Account shall be maintained in the name and style of “District Zilla Panchayat Fund” duly prefixing the name of Zilla Panchayat and subject to general control of Zilla Panchayat, shall be operated jointly by the Adhakshya and the Sachiva.

  • Provided that where a particular payment or drawal has been authorized and approved by the Secretary, Rural Development Department to do so as per the delegation of powers under these rules, Sachiva is authorized to sign cheques for payment for an amount not exceeding Rs. 50,000/-.

(4) The Zilla Panchayat may after passing a resolution in this behalf and after taking into account the liquidity position, invest part or whole of Zilla Panchayat fund in the Government approved securities and investment.

4. Maintenance of Cash Book. (1) It shall be the responsibility of the Sachiva to ensure maintenance of the Cash Book as specified in Schedule-A and in his safe custody. (2) All transactions shall be entered in Cash Book as soon as they occur in order of their occurrence. (3) The page of each book shall be numbered. The total number of pages in the book shall be certified by the Sachiva. (4) All receipts and payments shall be duly supported by vouchers which shall be numbered and their number, shall be recorded in the Cash Book. All receipt and payment vouchers shall be kept separately in the order of their number and date. (5) The cash book shall be closed on the last date of each calendar year or earlier and the balance shall be brought forward and shown as the opening on the first day of the next month. (6) An erasure or over writing of an entry in the cash book shall not be allowed. All incorrect entries shall be cancelled by drawing the pen once across the incorrect entry and inserting the correct one under the initials of concerned official.

5. Reconciliation of balance. (1) After the closure of Cash Book for a particular month, the Sachiva shall cause to be prepared, before the 15th of the following month, a statement of the receipt and expenditure during the month and the closing balance and a copy submitted in the form specified in Schedule-B. (2) In the event of non-reconciliation of the balance, Sachiva shall make all effort to find cause thereof and shall immediately bring this fact to the notice of Adhakshya and the Secretary.

6. Procedure for drawal of money from the Account. (1) No money shall be drawn from the Bank unless it is required for immediate disbursement. (2) All the expenses and payment for which the fund is proposed to be withdrawn should be duly approved and sanctioned by the Secretary, Rural Development Department under the provision of these rules or under the specific authorization of the Government. (3) All the drawals shall be made through cheque only after making the necessary entries in the cash book and recording details of payment on the counterfoil in the cheque. (4) A sum not exceeding Rs. 2000.00 (Rupees two thousand) only be kept as imprest fund with the official of the Zilla Panchayat duly authorized by the Sachiva. The total expenditure from the imprest fund shall be accounted for on the last working day of the month and sum equal of the total expenditure shall be transferred to imprest fund so as to make the closing balance equal to the opening balance of the month. However, the total expenditure on such petty accounts shall not exceed Rs. 5000/- annually out of imprest fund. A separate record shall be maintained for drawal of such imprest fund. (5) For grants/funds released by the State/Central Government, Utilization Certificate should invariably be submitted on quarterly basis to the Secretary, Rural Development Department. Failure to do so shall result in withholding of further grants/funds.

7. Preparation and maintenance of accounts. (1) The accounts of the Zilla Panchayat Funds shall be maintained in the manner specified in the rule and the various forms set out in the schedules. (2) It shall be mandatory for each Zilla Panchayat to prepare and maintain the following Books of Accounts and the Statement in the prescribed forms as specified in the schedules appended to these rules:

  • Cash Book
  • Ledger for recording item wise income and expenditure with the details of sub-head, minor and major head
  • Income and expenditure account and balance sheet
  • Inventory Register for fixed and non consumable assets and office equipments
  • Cheque drawn register

(3) In addition to major and minor heads, prescribed for preparation of Income and Expenditure Accounts and Annual Budget estimates of income and expenditure, the Zilla Panchayat may open suitable sub-heads under the minor heads concerned to exhibit the expenditure on different activities and income from different sources. No major and minor heads should, however, be added or removed without permission of the Secretary, Rural Development Department. (4) The Zilla Panchayat shall prepare at the end of each financial year a receipt and payment account, income and expenditure account and balance sheets to prepare a true and fair picture of the affairs of Zilla Panchayat and present the same to Zilla Panchayat in its meeting within three months from the closure of financial year and a copy of the same shall be submitted to Secretary.

  • Provided that the Secretary may allow a relaxation of two months for submission of actual accounts as above if he is satisfied that the delay has taken place due to unavoidable circumstances.

8. Preparation of Annual Budget and Estimates of Receipts & Payments. (1) Every Zilla Panchayat shall prepare in each year a budget of its estimated receipts and expenditure for the next financial year before the last day of August in the form specified in Schedule E and submit it to the Secretary for approval of the State Government.

  • Provided that before submission to the State Government final approval the budget shall first be placed before the Zilla Panchayat in its meeting for consideration and approval.

(2) The State Government shall approve the Budget of the Zilla Panchayat, with or without modification and such approval shall be communicated to Zilla Panchayat before the commencement of the next financial year. (3) No expenditure shall be incurred unless the Budget is approved by the State Government. (4) The Zilla Panchayat shall forward its proposal to the State Government for re-appropriation under one budget head and the State Government, after consideration of the proposal, may allow such re-appropriation subject to the conditions it may deem necessary to impose.

9. Authorities competent to sanction & incur expenditure & extent of their powers. (1) The power to incur expenditure out of the provision made in the approved budget and to the extent of such power shall be in accordance with the delegation of financial powers specified in Schedule - F. (2) The Zilla Panchayat shall in its meeting select 3 (three) members in addition to Adhakshaya and Up-Adhakshaya to form an Executive Committee which shall meet as often as desirable to discuss all matters other than routine administrative matters and also accord sanction wherever sanction is required to be accorded by the Executive Committee in accordance with the scheme of delegation of power specified in Schedule - F. The Executive Committee shall be responsible for supervision of all works and scheme of Zilla Panchayat. (3) Notwithstanding anything contained in this rule, the State Government may confer on any authority specific power to incur expenditure on specific items and upon such conferment, the said authority shall exercise such power for specified purpose only.

10. Custody, preservation of books and record & procedure for destruction. (1) The Sachiva shall ensure the safe custody of all the books of accounts and records of Zilla Panchayat and shall produce them for inspection to the auditors and any other officer/agency authorized by the Secretary. The following shall be the permanent record of Zilla Panchayat and shall be kept in record perpetually:

  • Cash Book
  • Bank Pass Book
  • Annual Accounts, viz., receipts and payments account of Income and Expenditure Statement and Balance Sheet
  • Audit Report
  • Assets Register

(2) Any other record not listed in sub-rule (1) above shall be preserved for the next 5 (five) years and thereafter, can be destroyed after their audit of accounts of that particular period has taken place as per the guidelines specified by the State Government in that behalf from time to time.

11. Audit of accounts. It shall be the duty of Adhakshya and Sachiva to get its accounts audited, whenever called upon to do so by the auditors appointed in its behalf by the State Government and extend all assistance to the auditors to facilitate examination of audit of accounts.

12. Removal of doubt. When a doubt arises as to the interpretation of any provision of these rules, the matter shall be referred to the Secretary, Rural Development Department whose decision thereon, shall be final and binding.

R. Ongmu,
Secretary to the Government of Sikkim,
Rural Development Department.

2004 Amendment Notification#

RURAL MANAGEMENT & DEVELOPMENT DEPARTMENT GOVERNMENT OF SIKKIM GANGTOK, SIKKIM

No. 35(30)2000-01/RDD/PII/18. Dated: 23.11.2004.

NOTIFICATION#

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 30 of the Sikkim Panchayat Act, 1993 (6 of 1993), the Governor of Sikkim is pleased to make the following rules further to amend the Sikkim Zilla Panchayat (Financial) Rules, 2001, namely:-

1. Short title and commencement. (1) These rules may be called the Sikkim Zilla Panchayat (Financial) Amendment Rules, 2004. (2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

2. Amendment of rule 3. In the Sikkim Zilla Panchayat (Financial) Rules, 2001, (hereinafter referred to as the said rules), the following shall be substituted, namely:-

“(3) The account shall be maintained in the name and style of District Zilla Panchayat Fund duly prefixing the name of the Zilla Panchayat and subject to the general control of the Zilla Panchayat and shall be operated by the Joint Director (Accounts) or any such Officer appointed by the State Government, by notification in the Official Gazette.”

3. Amendment of rule 6. In the said rules, in rule 6, -

(a) for sub-rule (2), the following shall be substituted, namely:-

“(2) All expenses and payments from the Zilla Panchayat Fund shall be in accordance with the approved budget of the Zilla Panchayat and the delegation of powers under Schedule F appended to the rules,”

(b) in sub rule (4), for the figure and words “Rs.2000.00 (Rupees two thousand)”, the figure and words “Rs.5000.00 (Rupees five thousand)” and for the figure and words “Rs.5000.00 (Rupees five thousand)”, the figure and words “Rs.10,000.00 (Rupees ten thousand)” shall, respectively be substituted.

V. B. Pathak, IAS,
Secretary to the Government of Sikkim,
Rural Management & Development Department.

# Building a Sikkim Pay Fixation Calculator: Understanding Rule 12 and Developing a Web-Based Promotion Pay Calculator

Government employees are often confused about how their pay is fixed after promotion. Although the Sikkim Government Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 2018 provide detailed provisions, manually calculating the revised pay can be time-consuming and prone to errors.

In this project, we built a web-based Pay Fixation Calculator that follows **Rule 12** of the Sikkim Revised Pay Rules, 2018 and uses the official Pay Matrix for automatic calculations.

The objectives were simple:

- Reduce calculation mistakes.
- Compare both fixation options instantly.

- Create a reusable tool for employees, auditors and DDOs.

# Understanding Rule 12

Rule 12 governs fixation of pay on promotion.

When an employee is promoted to a higher post, there are generally two choices:

## Option 1 – Fixation from Date of Promotion (DoP)

The employee opts to have pay fixed immediately from the date of promotion.

The calculation follows these steps:

1. Grant one increment in the existing Level.
2. Shift to the promoted Level.
3. Locate the cell equal to or immediately higher than the incremented pay.
4. That becomes the revised basic pay.

---

## Option 2 – Fixation from Date of Next Increment (DNI)

Instead of immediate fixation, the employee continues drawing pay in the old Level until the next increment.

On the Date of Next Increment:

- Normal annual increment is granted.
- Promotional increment is added.
- Pay is then fixed in the promoted Level.

This option often results in higher long-term benefits.

# Why Use JSON for the Pay Matrix?

Instead of hardcoding thousands of values into JavaScript, the matrix is stored separately.

```
pay-matrix.json
```

Advantages include:

- Easier maintenance
- Cleaner code
- Faster updates
- Reusable by other applications
- Easy integration with Python or Excel

---

# Sample JSON Structure

```json
{
  "11": [

    37900,
    39000,
    40200,
```

```javascript
fetch("pay-matrix.json")
```

# RTI Online Portal Training at NIC Sikkim

On **25 June 2025**, officials from various government departments attended a training programme conducted by the **National Informatics Centre (NIC), Sikkim** on the implementation and use of the **RTI Online Portal**.

The session focused on the complete lifecycle of an RTI application, user roles, workflow management, role assignment, and the responsibilities of various officers under the Right to Information Act.

This article summarizes the training and serves as a reference guide for future users of the RTI Online Portal.

---

# Objectives of the Training

The training was designed to help government officials:

- Understand the RTI Online Portal.
- Learn the workflow for processing RTI applications.
- Understand the responsibilities of different users.
- Learn how applications are assigned and processed.
- Familiarize themselves with role-based access control.
- Ensure compliance with the guidelines issued by the Central Information Commission (CIC) and the Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT).

---

# About the RTI Online Portal

The RTI Online Portal enables citizens to submit RTI applications electronically and allows government departments to process those applications digitally.

The platform has been developed by **NIC** following the guidelines prescribed by:

- Central Information Commission (CIC)
- Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT)

The system replaces much of the manual paperwork with an organized digital workflow.

# User Roles in the RTI Portal

The portal uses a role-based access system.

Each user receives permissions according to their assigned role.

The major roles discussed during the training were:

- Nodal SPIO
- SPIO
- ASPIO
- First Appellate Authority
- Consultant (if applicable)

---

# Understanding the Nodal SPIO

One of the SPIOs in the department is designated as the **Nodal SPIO**.

This is one of the most important roles in the portal.

The Nodal SPIO acts as the administrator for the department.

Their responsibilities include:

- Managing RTI users
- Assigning applications
- Creating workflow
- Assigning roles
- Monitoring RTI cases

---

# Responsibilities of the Nodal SPIO

The Nodal SPIO can:

- Assign RTI applications.
- Register users.
- Assign roles.
- Change user roles.
- Allocate applications to concerned SPIOs.
- Allocate work to ASPIOs.
- Configure departmental workflow.

Only the Nodal SPIO has these administrative privileges.

---

# Login Process

During the first login:

- Username is provided by the administrator.
- Password is sent through email.
- Users are required to change the password after the first login.

This ensures secure access to the RTI portal.

---

# Role Assignment

One of the major topics covered during the training was role assignment.

Only the **Nodal SPIO** can assign roles.

Roles that can be assigned include:

- SPIO
- ASPIO
- First Appellate Authority
- Consultant
- Other authorized users

Ordinary users cannot change or assign roles.

# RTI Application Workflow

The portal follows a structured workflow.

```text
Citizen
      │
      ▼
RTI Application Submitted
      │
      ▼
Nodal SPIO
      │
      ▼
Assigns Application
      │
      ▼
SPIO / ASPIO
      │
      ▼
Prepare Reply
      │
      ▼
SPIO Reviews
      │
      ▼
Reply Sent to Applicant
````

This ensures accountability and transparency.

# Assigning RTI Tasks

Whenever a new RTI application is received:

1. The application reaches the department.
2. The Nodal SPIO reviews it.
3. The application is assigned to the appropriate SPIO or ASPIO.
4. The assigned officer processes the request.
5. A reply is prepared.

The portal keeps track of every stage.

Each action is recorded within the portal.

# Important Difference Between SPIO and ASPIO

One of the most important concepts discussed during the session was the distinction between the SPIO and ASPIO.

## ASPIO

The ASPIO can:

* Examine the RTI application.
* Collect information.
* Draft a reply.

However:

**The ASPIO cannot send the reply directly to the applicant.**

---

## SPIO

The SPIO has the authority to:

* Review the draft reply.
* Approve it.
* Send the final response to the applicant.

Therefore, every reply prepared by an ASPIO must first reach the SPIO.

Only after approval can it be dispatched.

---

# Why Only SPIO Can Send Replies

This workflow ensures:

* Proper accountability.
* Legal compliance.
* Departmental approval.
* Consistent communication.
* Official responsibility under the RTI Act.

The SPIO remains legally responsible for the information supplied.

# Common Workflow Example

Suppose a citizen submits an RTI application.

The process would be:

1. Citizen submits the RTI online.
2. Department receives the application.
3. Nodal SPIO assigns it.
4. ASPIO gathers information.
5. ASPIO prepares the draft reply.
6. SPIO reviews the draft.
7. SPIO sends the reply.
8. Applicant receives the official response.

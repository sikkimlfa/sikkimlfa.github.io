# Comprehensive Guide to Sikkim Government Financial, Travelling Allowance, Vehicle, and Service Rules

Navigating the administrative, financial, and service regulations of the Government of Sikkim requires a thorough understanding of various notifications, rules, and statutory amendments issued by the Finance, Revenue and Expenditure Department (FRED), the Transport Department, the Department of Personnel (DoPT), and the High Court of Sikkim. 

This comprehensive blog post synthesizes the critical insights across key administrative domains, including **Travelling Allowance (TA) Rules**, **Pay Matrix & Judicial Allowances**, **Vehicle Procurement and Maintenance Regulations**, **Transport Fares**, and **Police Manual Guidelines**.

---

## 1. Sikkim Travelling Allowance (TA) Rules and Amendments

Travelling allowances for Sikkim Government servants are governed by the foundational **Sikkim Travelling Allowances Rules, 1980**, along with subsequent amendments (such as the 5th Amendment Rules 2006, the 2012 Amendments, and the 2016 Notifications).

### Classification of Government Servants
Under the revised grading structure linked to Grade Pay:
* **Grade I:** Posts having a Grade Pay of ₹8,700 and above.
* **Grade II:** Posts having a Grade Pay of ₹7,200.
* **Grade III:** Posts having a Grade Pay of ₹5,000 to ₹6,700.
* **Grade IV:** Posts having a Grade Pay of ₹3,400 to ₹4,600.
* **Grade V:** Posts having a Grade Pay of ₹3,000 and below.

### Journey on Tour and Entitlements
* **Rail Travel:** * *First Grade:* A.C. First Class / A.C. 2-Tier.
  * *Second Grade:* A.C. Chair Car / First Class / A.C. 3-Tier / Sleeper / Second Class.
  * *Third & Fourth Grades:* Sleeper / Second Class.
  * *Reservation Charges:* Reimbursable for day and night journeys across all grades.
* **Road Travel within Sikkim:** Governed by official tariffs prescribed by the Sikkim Nationalised Transport (SNT) or the Sikkim Transport Authority (STA).
* **Air Travel (Medical Treatment):** Government servants drawing a Grade Pay of ₹7,200 and above are entitled to travel by Air in economy class when referred outside the State for medical treatment as a patient.

### Journey on Transfer, Retirement, or Death
* **Transfer Grants & Packing Allowances:** Lump-sum transfer grants and packing allowances vary by grade (e.g., First Grade down to Fourth Grade) to cover the carriage of personal effects by goods train or approved motor transport.
* **Retirement / Death in Service:** Families of deceased employees or retiring personnel are entitled to actual motor fares, rail fares, and transportation costs for personal effects, provided the journey is completed within the stipulated timeframe (typically 6 months).

## 2. Tour Program Approval Procedures (Standing Order No. 140/Fin/Adm)

Strict protocols govern the sanction and approval of official tours within and outside Sikkim:
* **Tours Within Sikkim:**
  * Heads of Departments (HoDs) seek approval from their respective Ministries, copying the Chief Minister and Chief Secretary.
  * Gangtok-based Class I officers require approval from their HoD.
  * District officers and heads of field offices seek approval from the respective District Collector (acting as Controlling Officer).
* **Tours Outside Sikkim:**
  * HoDs must obtain the prior approval of the Chief Minister through the Chief Secretary and the Minister-in-Charge.
  * Non-gazetted staff require approval through their designated Head of Office and HoD.

## 3. Pay Matrix & Judicial Officers' Allowances

### Sikkim Government Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 2018
The pay structure is organized into a standardized **Pay Matrix** corresponding to the rationalized levels matching pre-revised Pay Bands and Grade Pays, ensuring uniformity across state departments.

### Judicial Officers (Allowances, Amenities, and Advances) Rules, 2004 & Leave Rules
Aligned with Supreme Court directives and National Judicial Pay Commission recommendations:
* **Dearness Allowance (DA):** Matches the formula applied to Central Government employees.
* **Sumptuary Allowance:** * *District Judge & equivalent:* ₹3,100/- per month.
  * *Civil Judge-cum-Chief Judicial Magistrate:* ₹2,300/- per month.
* **Leave Travel Concession (LTC) / Home Travel Concession (HTC):** Regulated under specific High Court of Sikkim notifications to facilitate smooth transit and leave encashment guidelines.

## 4. Vehicle Procurement, Maintenance, and Transport Fares

### Sikkim Government Vehicle Procurement and Maintenance Rules, 2025
Notification No. 03/FIN/ADM (dated 23/07/2025) strictly regulates the acquisition, deployment, and financial ceilings for vehicle repairs:
* Annual expenditure caps and cumulative repair cost ceilings are enforced based on the vehicle type (Two-wheelers, Utility vehicles, Pick-up vans, Heavy vehicles, and Pool vehicles).
* Technical inspections are mandatory to certify roadworthiness before major maintenance expenditures are sanctioned.

### Sikkim Cab App Fares (Transport Department Notification)
Official tiered fare structures for local taxis, motor cabs, maxi cabs, and luxury vehicles under the 'Sikkim Cab' app platform define transparent distance and time-based slabs (e.g., fixed slabs for initial kilometers followed by per-kilometer rates for micro, mini, utility, and luxury categories).

## 1. Statutory Checklist for Zilla Panchayat Financial Audits

[cite_start]When auditing the accounts of a Zilla Panchayat, the primary focus rests on verifying compliance with state-specific regulations—such as the **Sikkim Zilla Panchayat (Financial) Rules** [cite: 3][cite_start]—alongside the **C&AG Financial Audit Guidelines for PRIs**[cite: 124, 134].

### I. Maintenance of Books and Cash Book
* [cite_start]**Format Compliance**: Verify if the Cash Book is maintained strictly in the prescribed format (e.g., Schedule A)[cite: 12, 95].
* [cite_start]**Chronological Recording**: Ensure all transactions are recorded in order of occurrence as soon as they take place[cite: 12, 95].
* [cite_start]**Custody and Page Certification**: Confirm each page is machine-numbered and that the total page count is certified on the flyleaf by the **Sachiva**[cite: 12, 13, 95].
* [cite_start]**Voucher Support**: Check that every entry (receipt or payment) is supported by an authentically numbered voucher[cite: 13, 54, 735].
* [cite_start]**Rectification Protocols**: Ensure no erasures or overwriting exist[cite: 14]. [cite_start]Incorrect entries must be drawn across with a single line and corrected under the official's initials[cite: 15].
* [cite_start]**Periodic Closure**: Confirm the Cash Book is closed regularly on the last working date of each calendar month[cite: 14, 96].

### II. Zilla Panchayat Fund & Bank Operations
* [cite_start]**Authorized Depository**: Confirm the Zilla Panchayat Fund is maintained in a Savings Bank Account with the State Bank of Sikkim or a designated nationalized bank at the District Headquarter/Gangtok[cite: 7, 88].
* [cite_start]**Joint Operation**: Ensure the account is operated under the joint signatures of the **Adhakshya** and the **Sachiva** (or designated financial officer)[cite: 9, 91, 266].
* [cite_start]**Prompt Receipts**: Check that all monies collected are deposited into the bank on the same day or the next working day[cite: 8, 89]. [cite_start]Cash or cheques must not be retained beyond statutory limits without express approval[cite: 90].
* [cite_start]**Cheque Security**: Verify the Cheque Drawn Register and check that unissued cheque books remain under lock and key in safe custody[cite: 25, 56].

### III. Budgetary Control & Expenditure Sanctions
* [cite_start]**Timely Preparation**: Confirm the annual budget estimate of receipts and expenditure was prepared prior to the statutory deadline (e.g., end of August)[cite: 30, 107].
* [cite_start]**Panchayat Approval**: Verify that the draft budget was formally presented and approved in a Zilla Panchayat meeting before submission to the State Government[cite: 31, 108].
* [cite_start]**Prior Approval Requirement**: Ensure no expenditure was incurred prior to formal budget approval by the State Government[cite: 32, 110].
* [cite_start]**Executive Committee**: Verify that an Executive Committee (comprising the Adhakshya, Up-Adhakshya, and three elected members) was constituted to accord necessary financial sanctions as per delegated powers[cite: 34, 116].

### IV. Financial Statements & Reconciliation
* [cite_start]**Monthly Reconciliation**: Confirm the Sachiva prepares a monthly bank reconciliation statement comparing the Cash Book balance against the Bank Pass Book/Statement[cite: 96, 379].
* [cite_start]**Annual Financial Statements**: Verify the preparation and presentation of mandatory annual statements within three months of the financial year closure[cite: 28, 104]:
  * [cite_start]Receipt and Payment Account [cite: 28, 104]
  * [cite_start]Income and Expenditure Account [cite: 25, 28, 104]
  * [cite_start]Balance Sheet [cite: 25, 28, 104]
  * [cite_start]General and Minor-Head Ledgers [cite: 24, 102]

### V. Asset Management, Imprest & Records Preservation
* [cite_start]**Inventory Control**: Inspect the Inventory Register for fixed assets, non-consumable goods, and office equipment[cite: 25, 102].
* [cite_start]**Imprest Fund Limits**: Ensure the petty cash imprest fund stays within prescribed ceilings (e.g., maximum limits of Rs. 2,000–Rs. 5,000) and annual caps are strictly respected[cite: 17, 19, 98, 267].
* [cite_start]**Perpetual Records**: Confirm permanent records—such as Cash Books, Bank Pass Books, Annual Accounts, and Audit Reports—are kept perpetually in safe custody[cite: 39, 120, 121]. [cite_start]Non-permanent records must be preserved for at least five years post-audit[cite: 121].

## 2. Head-Wise Operational Audit Methodologies

[cite_start]Auditing broad expenditure heads requires specific substantive testing techniques to ensure propriety, regularity, and accuracy[cite: 188, 407, 409].

| Expenditure Head | Key Focus Area | Primary Audit Check |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Works & Infrastructure** | Approvals & Measurements | [cite_start]Verify administrative approvals, technical sanctions, and test-check entries in the Measurement Book against sanctioned estimates[cite: 49, 454]. |
| **Supplies & Procurements** | Stores & Accounting | [cite_start]Confirm physical delivery verification certificates on bills and trace indents directly to the Stationery/Stores Register[cite: 422, 712]. |
| **Contracts & Agreements** | Tendering & Terms | [cite_start]Review comparative tender statements, competitive bidding procedures, and ensure no contract terms were modified post-award without sanction[cite: 401, 464, 946]. |

## 3. Auditing General Administrative Expenditure

General administrative expenses represent the daily operational overhead of an institution. Auditing these items relies on enforcing internal control mechanisms and verification trails.

+------------------+      +-------------------+      +-------------------+
|

|  Reconciliation   |

### Key Administrative Controls
1. [cite_start]**Voucher Cancellation**: Every payment voucher must be physically or digitally stamped **"PAID AND CANCELLED"** upon settlement to prevent double-dipping or duplicate submission[cite: 741].
2. [cite_start]**Staffing Sanctions**: Pay bills must be checked against sanctioned post strengths to ensure no temporary or unauthorized personnel are paid under administrative heads[cite: 421, 940].
3. **Vehicle Logbook Audit**: Logbooks must be checked for complete entries—including trip start/end times, exact mileage, destination, officer authorization, and fuel receipt entries.
4. [cite_start]**Periodic Stock Verification**: Stock ledgers should undergo independent, surprise physical checks at least once annually, certified by a non-custodial officer[cite: 348, 904].

## 4. In-Depth Focus: Auditing the Stationery Stock Register

A critical micro-check during administrative audits is verifying the **Stationery Stock Register**. [cite_start]Unmonitored office supplies represent a common source of petty leakage and financial waste[cite: 393].

+-----------------------+       +------------------------+       +-----------------------+
| Requisition / Indent  | ----> | Sanction by Authorized | ----> | Stock Register Issue  |
| Raised by Branch Head |       | Officer / Supervisor   |       | Entry & Recalculation |
+-----------------------+       +------------------------+       +-----------------------+
|
v
+-----------------------+
| Physical Verification |
| & Balance Cross-Check |
+-----------------------+

```

### Core Audit Steps for Issue Verification
* **Mandatory Written Indents**: Confirm that no stationery item is issued based on verbal requests. [cite_start]Every issue entry in the stock ledger must correspond to an explicit, written requisition/indent signed by the receiving branch head[cite: 422].
* [cite_start]**Cross-Checking Quantities**: Compare the quantity requested in the indent against the quantity marked as "Issued" in the ledger, verifying that the receiver's acknowledgment signature is recorded[cite: 425].
* [cite_start]**Receipt-to-Invoice Traceability**: Reconcile all "Receipt" entries with original press invoices, delivery challans, or supply bills to ensure complete accounting of incoming stock[cite: 422, 779].
* [cite_start]**Arithmetical Accuracy**: Test-check running balances across pages to confirm that opening balances plus receipts minus issues equal the closing balance[cite: 340, 365].

### Red Flags to Watch For
* **Unsigned Requisitions**: Supplies issued without written approval signal a collapse of internal access controls.
* **Non-Chronological Postings**: Backdated stock entries often indicate retroactive ledger writing to mask stock shortages or unaccounted usage.
* [cite_start]**Unauthenticated Alterations**: Erasures or white-out corrections in the balance or issue columns without initialing suggest potential manipulation[cite: 14, 15].
* [cite_start]**Static Balances**: Stores showing constant closing balances over long periods despite active office operations indicate that stock books are not being updated in real time[cite: 420].
* [cite_start]**Surprise Physical Audit**: Auditors should select high-value items (e.g., printer cartridges, paper reams) for a physical count, comparing actual physical balances directly against the register's recorded closing balance[cite: 348].

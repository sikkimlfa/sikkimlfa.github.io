### Sikkim Local Fund Audit Directorate

The Directorate of Local Fund Audit operates under the Finance Department, Government of Sikkim. It serves as the statutory audit authority responsible for overseeing the financial operations, accounts, and statutory audit checks of local bodies and assigned state institutions across Sikkim.

---

### Core Objectives & Responsibilities

* **Statutory Compliance:** Ensuring local fund receipts and expenditures align with statutory acts, financial codes, and subsidiary treasury regulations.
* **Gram Panchayat & Urban Local Bodies (ULB) Audit:** Conducting periodic financial reviews and expenditure tracking of local self-government institutions.
* **Assigned Funds & Grants:** Scrutinizing utilization certificates, special project allocations, and central/state sponsored program expenditures.
* **Audit Reports & Advisory:** Formulating annual audit inspection reports, issuing corrective audit observations, and assisting administrative authorities in plugging financial leakage.

---

### Legal & Regulatory Mandate

Audits and accounts monitoring are conducted in accordance with:
* The Sikkim Local Fund Audit Act and corresponding State Financial Rules.
* Government notifications, guidelines, and circulars issued by the Finance Department.
* Applicable standards on public sector accounts and expenditure verifications.

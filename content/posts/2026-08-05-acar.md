# Drafting Office Notings and Official Letters for Submission of the Annual Consolidated Audit Report

Government correspondence demands clarity, precision, and compliance with statutory provisions. One such important communication is the submission of the **Annual Consolidated Audit Report (ACAR)** to the State Legislature.

Under the **Sikkim Local Fund Audit Act, 2012**, the Directorate of Local Fund Audit is required to prepare and submit the Annual Consolidated Audit Report for laying before the State Legislature. This process generally involves preparing an office noting seeking approval, forwarding authenticated and printed copies to the Legislative Assembly Secretariat, and uploading electronic copies wherever prescribed.

This article explains the purpose of these documents and provides model drafts that may be adapted for official use.

---

# Legal Background

Section **19** of the **Sikkim Local Fund Audit Act, 2012** provides that the Annual Consolidated Audit Report shall be submitted to the Government for laying before the State Legislature.

Before every Assembly Session, the Legislative Assembly Secretariat generally communicates the schedule for the forthcoming session and requests submission of reports required to be laid before the House.

For the report relating to the financial year **2023–24**, the Secretariat informed that the Assembly had been convened on **30 June 2025** vide **Letter No. 411/L&PA/SLAS dated 18.06.2025**.

Accordingly, the following documents were required to be submitted:

- Two authenticated copies of the Annual Consolidated Audit Report.
- Forty printed copies.
- Soft copies in PDF and DOC formats uploaded online.

---

# Purpose of the Office Noting

The office noting serves multiple purposes:

- Records the statutory requirement.
- Refers to the communication received from the Legislative Assembly Secretariat.
- Indicates the documents prepared for submission.
- Seeks approval from the competent authority before dispatch.

A well-drafted noting should be concise, factual, and avoid unnecessary narration.

---

# Model Office Noting

## Subject

**Submission of Annual Consolidated Audit Report for the year ended 2023–24 to the Sikkim Legislative Assembly**

---

It is submitted that, under the provisions of **Section 19 of the Sikkim Local Fund Audit Act, 2012**, the **Annual Consolidated Audit Report** for the year ended **2023–24** is required to be laid before the State Legislature.

In this connection, the **Sikkim Legislative Assembly Secretariat**, vide **Letter No. 411/L&PA/SLAS dated 18.06.2025**, has informed that the Assembly has been convened on **30 June 2025**.

Accordingly, the following documents have been prepared for submission:

1. Two authenticated copies of the Annual Consolidated Audit Report for the year ended 2023–24.
2. Forty printed copies of the report.
3. Soft copies of the report in PDF and DOC formats for uploading on the designated online portal.

Approval is solicited to forward the authenticated copies, printed copies, and upload the electronic versions for submission to the **Sikkim Legislative Assembly Secretariat** for laying before the Legislature.

**Submitted please.**

**Accounts Officer**

---

# Why Two Authenticated Copies?

Authenticated copies are officially signed and certified copies of the report.

These copies serve as the official record maintained by the Legislative Assembly Secretariat and other designated authorities.

---

# Why Forty Printed Copies?

Printed copies are generally supplied for:

- Hon'ble Speaker
- Hon'ble Members of the Legislative Assembly
- Legislative Assembly Secretariat
- Parliamentary Branch
- Record Sections
- Government Departments concerned

The exact number depends upon the directions issued by the Secretariat.

---

# Importance of Soft Copies

Most legislatures now maintain digital repositories of reports.

Soft copies in both **PDF** and **Word (DOC)** formats enable:

- Electronic record management.
- Online publication where permitted.
- Easy archival.
- Searchability.
- Future reference.

# Model Forwarding Letter

**No. ________/DLFA/ACAR/2025**

**Government of Sikkim**

**Directorate of Local Fund Audit**

Gangtok

Date: __________

## To

The Secretary,

Sikkim Legislative Assembly Secretariat,

Gangtok – 737101.

### Subject: Submission of Annual Consolidated Audit Report for the year ended 2023–24 under Section 19 of the Sikkim Local Fund Audit Act, 2012.

Sir,

I am directed to forward herewith the **Annual Consolidated Audit Report** for the year ended **2023–24** in compliance with the provisions of **Section 19 of the Sikkim Local Fund Audit Act, 2012**, for laying before the Hon'ble State Legislature.

As requested vide **Letter No. 411/L&PA/SLAS dated 18.06.2025**, the following are enclosed:

1. Two authenticated copies of the Annual Consolidated Audit Report.
2. Forty printed copies of the report.

Further, soft copies of the report in **PDF** and **DOC** formats have been uploaded online as required.

It is requested that the receipt of the above documents may kindly be acknowledged.

Yours faithfully,

**Accounts Officer**

Directorate of Local Fund Audit

Government of Sikkim

# Suggested Dispatch Checklist

Before dispatching the report, verify the following:

- Report approved by the competent authority.
- Authentication completed.
- All annexures included.
- Forty printed copies counted.
- Dispatch number allotted.
- Covering letter signed.
- PDF uploaded.
- DOC uploaded.
- Acknowledgement requested.
- Dispatch entered in the register.

---

# Best Practices for Government Drafting

When preparing office notings and forwarding letters:

- Quote the relevant statutory provision accurately.
- Mention the Assembly communication with its letter number and date.
- Clearly specify the number of copies enclosed.
- Mention electronic submission separately.
- End the office noting with "Submitted please."
- Request acknowledgement in the forwarding letter.
- Maintain concise and formal language.

---

# Conclusion

Submission of the Annual Consolidated Audit Report is a statutory responsibility that supports legislative oversight of local bodies. Proper documentation through an office noting and a formal forwarding letter ensures procedural compliance, accountability, and timely placement of the report before the Legislature.

Using standardized formats also improves consistency in government correspondence and facilitates efficient record management.

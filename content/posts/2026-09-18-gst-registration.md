# How DDOs Obtain GST Registration for TDS in Sikkim

A Drawing and Disbursing Officer (DDO) does not obtain GST-TDS registration in a personal capacity. Instead, the Government department, office, Gram Panchayat, Zilla Panchayat, Municipality or other notified deductor obtains registration, while the DDO or authorised signatory completes the application on behalf of that organisation.

The application is submitted electronically in Form GST REG-07 through the official GST portal. Rule 12 of the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Rules, 2017 governs this procedure, and the GST portal's official user guide includes a dedicated Drawing and Disbursing Officer section in the application.

> **Important:** Confirm the current registration procedure with the Sikkim Commercial Taxes Division, the Finance Department and the GST portal before submitting the application.

---

## 1. When GST-TDS Registration Is Required

Registration is mandatory when an organisation is liable to deduct tax at source under Section 51 of the CGST Act, 2017 and corresponding provisions of the Sikkim Goods and Services Tax (SGST) Act, 2017.

Potential deductors include:

- Sikkim Government departments.
- Subordinate Government offices.
- Gram Panchayats.
- Zilla Panchayats.
- Municipalities and Municipal Corporations.
- Governmental agencies.
- Other notified public bodies.

GST-TDS registration is completely separate from regular GST registration as a supplier.

| Situation | Registration Requirement |
|---|---|
| Office only receives goods or services | GST-TDS registration does not automatically arise unless Section 51 applies |
| Office is liable to deduct GST-TDS | GST registration as a tax deductor is compulsory under Section 24(vi) |
| Office makes taxable outward supplies | Regular GST registration as a supplier may be required |
| DDO personally processes bills | DDO does not register personally; the organisation registers |

---

## 2. Statutory Forms

The application and issuance cycle uses the following statutory forms:

- **Form GST REG-07:** Application for registration as a tax deductor under Section 51 or tax collector at source under Section 52.
- **Form GST REG-06:** Certificate of registration issued by the proper officer upon approval.
- **Form GST REG-08:** Order of cancellation of registration when the entity ceases to be liable to deduct tax at source.

---

## 3. Authorised Applicants and Portal Architecture

The application is submitted by:

- The Drawing and Disbursing Officer.
- The Head of Office.
- The designated primary authorised signatory.
- Another officer formally authorised by the department or local body.

The applicant must provide:

- An official Government email address.
- An official Government mobile number.
- The exact legal name of the entity.
- The correct Permanent Account Number (PAN) or Tax Deduction and Collection Account Number (TAN).
- The physical office address.
- A valid administrative authorisation order.
- Authentication via Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) or Electronic Verification Code (EVC).

The GST portal user guide divides the Form GST REG-07 application into five distinct tabs:

1. Business Details.
2. Drawing and Disbursing Officer.
3. Authorised Signatory.
4. Office Address of the Tax Deductor.
5. Verification.

## 4. Required Documents and Information

Before starting the application, assemble the required organisation, officer and premises documents.

### 4.1 Organisation Details

- Legal name of the department, directorate or local authority.
- PAN of the entity (where applicable).
- TAN of the deductor (mandatory or alternative identifier for deductors).
- Entity classification (Government Department, Local Authority, Statutory Body).
- State: Sikkim.
- District and administrative jurisdiction.
- Official contact details and physical address.

### 4.2 DDO Details

- Full legal name.
- Father's or parent's name.
- Date of birth and gender.
- Official designation.
- PAN of the DDO.
- Residential address with PIN code.
- Official telephone number and email address.
- Recent passport-sized photograph (JPEG format, within prescribed file-size limits).
- Identity proof and authentication credentials (Aadhaar or DSC).

### 4.3 Authorised Signatory Details

- Full name and official designation.
- PAN.
- Mobile number and email address.
- Formal departmental authorisation order.
- Valid Class 3 DSC registered on the GST portal (if filing via DSC).

### 4.4 Address Proof

Acceptable documents showing possession of premises include:

- Government office allotment order.
- Departmental address certificate issued by a competent authority.
- Property ownership record or municipal tax receipt.
- Registered rent or lease agreement.
- Recent electricity bill in the name of the office.

Organise electronic uploads using a consistent naming format:

## 5. Preliminary Preparation

Complete five preparatory steps before filing online:

### Step 1: Confirm legal liability

Obtain written confirmation from the Finance Department, controlling directorate, Sikkim Commercial Taxes Division or legal cell verifying that the office is required to deduct tax under Section 51.

### Step 2: Define the registering entity

Determine whether the registration applies to:

* The State department at large.
* A specific directorate.
* A district-level office.
* A Municipality or Municipal Corporation.
* A Gram Panchayat Unit or Zilla Panchayat.

Never register a DDO in an individual personal capacity.

### Step 3: Check existing registrations

Search the GST portal using the entity's PAN or TAN to verify if an existing GSTIN, provisional ID, Unique Identity Number (UIN) or GST-TDS registration is already active in Sikkim.

### Step 4: Formally nominate the authorised signatory

Issue an administrative office order nominating:

* The primary authorised signatory.
* An alternate officer (if required).
* The DDO responsible for record maintenance.
* The officer responsible for monthly Form GSTR-7 filings.
* The officer responsible for register reconciliation.

### Step 5: Establish a compliance calendar

Record key milestones:

* Application submission date and Application Reference Number (ARN).
* Target approval date.
* GSTIN receipt date.
* Monthly tax deposit deadline (10th of the following month).
* Form GSTR-7 filing deadline.
* Form GSTR-7A monitoring schedule.
* Annual compliance review date.

## 6. Online Registration Procedure: Part A

1. Navigate to the official GST common portal (`https://www.gst.gov.in`).
2. Go to **Services** → **Registration** → **New Registration**.
3. In the **I am a** drop-down field, select **Tax Deductor**.
4. In the **State / UT** field, select **Sikkim**.
5. Select the appropriate **District**.
6. Enter the **Legal Name of the Tax Deductor** exactly as recorded in the Income Tax PAN or TAN database.
7. Enter the **PAN** or **TAN** of the entity.
8. Provide the **Official Email Address** and **Official Mobile Number**.
9. Enter the CAPTCHA characters and select **Proceed**.
10. Enter the separate One-Time Passwords (OTPs) sent to the mobile number and email address.
11. Select **Continue** to generate the 15-digit Temporary Reference Number (TRN).

> **Important:** Always use an official institutional email address and mobile number. Avoid personal contact details that become inaccessible upon an officer's transfer or superannuation.

---

## 7. Completing Part B Using the TRN

1. Return to the GST portal and select **Services** → **Registration** → **New Registration**.
2. Select the **Temporary Reference Number (TRN)** option.
3. Enter the TRN and CAPTCHA, then click **Proceed**.
4. Enter the mobile and email OTPs to access the dashboard.
5. In the **My Saved Applications** panel, select the **Edit** (pencil) icon under the Action column.
6. Complete all tabs in Part B within the prescribed statutory window before the TRN expires.

---

## 8. Business Details Tab

Provide the administrative and jurisdictional details:

* **Trade Name:** Operational title of the office or establishment.
* **Legal Name:** Pre-populated from Part A.
* **Constitution of Business:** Select Government Department, Local Authority or Statutory Body.
* **Deductor Type:** Specify the appropriate category.
* **Jurisdiction:** Select the appropriate State jurisdiction (Sikkim Commercial Taxes Division ward/circle) and Central jurisdiction (Commissionerate, Division and Range).

### Legal Name Matching

Ensure the legal name matches the PAN or TAN records precisely. For example:

* **Incorrect:** `Rural Office`
* **Correct:** `Office of the Additional District Collector, Development, Gangtok, Sikkim`

Discrepancies between portal entries and Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) records will trigger automated validation errors.

---

## 9. Drawing and Disbursing Officer Tab

The DDO tab captures data for the officer accountable for financial transactions:

* Full legal name and father's or parent's name.
* Date of birth and gender.
* Official designation (e.g., Drawing and Disbursing Officer, Accounts Officer).
* Personal PAN of the DDO.
* Residential address with district and PIN code.
* Official contact details.
* Uploaded passport-sized photograph (JPEG format, file size between 20 KB and 100 KB).

Retain copies of the DDO's formal posting order and delegation of financial powers in the office registration file.

---

## 10. Authorised Signatory Tab

If the DDO also serves as the primary authorised signatory:

1. Enable the checkbox indicating **Primary Authorised Signatory**.
2. Confirm that personal details automatically populate from the DDO tab.
3. Upload the formal departmental authorisation letter or office order.

If another officer acts as authorised signatory:

1. Manually enter the officer's personal and designation details.
2. Provide their official contact credentials.
3. Upload the formal departmental authorisation resolution or office order.
4. Upload their identity proof and photograph.

---

## 11. Office Address of the Tax Deductor Tab

Record the physical location of the establishment:

* Building number or name, street and locality.
* State: Sikkim.
* District and PIN code.
* Official telephone, mobile and institutional email address.
* **Nature of possession of premises:** Select Own, Leased, Rented, Shared or Other.
* **Proof of Office Address:** Upload a valid Government allotment order, property ownership document, electricity bill or lease deed (PDF or JPEG format, maximum 1 MB).

---

## 12. Verification and Final Submission

1. Navigate to the **Verification** tab.
2. Check the statutory declaration box confirming the accuracy of all submitted details.
3. Select the name of the **Authorised Signatory** from the drop-down list.
4. Enter the **Place** of signing (e.g., Gangtok, Namchi, Gyalshing, Mangan).
5. Submit the application using an approved authentication method:
* **Submit with DSC:** Mandatory for public limited companies and recommended for Government entities holding an active Class 3 DSC.
* **Submit with EVC:** Validates via OTP sent to the registered mobile number and email address of the authorised signatory.


6. Upon successful submission, the system generates an Application Reference Number (ARN) within 15 minutes.
7. Download and preserve the official acknowledgement receipt.

## 14. Responding to Clarification Notices

If the proper officer issues a notice in Form GST REG-03 seeking clarification or additional documentation:

1. Review the specific query raised on the GST portal immediately.
2. Prepare a formal reply in Form GST REG-04 within seven working days.
3. Obtain written approval from the Head of Office before submission.
4. Upload clear, legible supporting documents resolving the query.
5. Track the application status on the portal until approved.

Avoid uploading generic explanations, submitting mismatched PAN/TAN documents or altering the organisation's legal name without formal justification.

## 15. Operational Steps Following GSTIN Issuance

Upon approval of Form GST REG-07, the proper officer issues a registration certificate in Form GST REG-06 containing the 15-digit GSTIN.

The DDO must immediately:

1. Download Form GST REG-06 from the portal.
2. Confirm that the legal name, constitution, office address and taxpayer type (*Tax Deductor*) are accurate.
3. Note the effective date of registration.
4. Update the departmental accounting and billing software.
5. Notify the State Treasury, Pay and Accounts Office (PAO) and internal accounts branches.
6. Issue an internal circular containing the newly allocated deductor GSTIN.

### Model Internal Circular

```text
GOVERNMENT OF SIKKIM
[NAME OF DEPARTMENT / LOCAL BODY]
GANGTOK, SIKKIM

Office Order No: [Number]                                   Dated: [YYYY-MM-DD]

SUBJECT: ALLOTMENT OF GSTIN FOR GST-TDS COMPLIANCE UNDER SECTION 51

It is hereby notified that this office has obtained registration as a Tax Deductor
under Section 51 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 and the Sikkim
Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017.

1. Deductor GSTIN: [11XXXXXXXXXXD1X]
2. Legal Name: [Official Registered Name]
3. Effective Date of Registration: [YYYY-MM-DD]
4. Designated Nodal Officer (DDO): [Name and Designation]
5. Form GSTR-7 Filing In-Charge: [Name and Designation]

All bill-scrutiny sections, accounts staff and engineering wings shall verify GST-TDS
applicability on all works and procurement contracts exceeding ₹2,50,000 (excluding
taxes). Deduction details must be submitted to the accounts section to ensure monthly
remittance and filing by the 10th of each succeeding month.

[Signature]
Head of Department / Designated Authority

```

## 16. Monthly Compliance Workflow

Registration is the prerequisite for an ongoing, cyclic compliance workflow.

```text
Receive Bill & Verify Contract Value (> ₹2,50,000 excluding taxes)
                          ↓
Calculate Deductions (1% CGST + 1% SGST or 2% IGST)
                          ↓
Pass Net Payment & Record in GST-TDS Register
                          ↓
Deposit Withheld Tax to Government Account (by the 10th)
                          ↓
File Form GSTR-7 Electronically (by the 10th)
                          ↓
Ensure Form GSTR-7A Generation for Deductees
                          ↓
Perform Monthly Three-Way Ledger Reconciliation

```

## 18. Interaction with Existing Regular GST Registrations

Regular supplier GST registration and GST-TDS registration serve different statutory purposes.

A Government office that supplies taxable services (such as commercial renting or commercial testing) may already hold a regular supplier GSTIN. However, if the department also acts as a public authority deducting tax under Section 51, it must obtain a distinct tax-deductor registration.

* Review existing registrations linked to the department's PAN on the GST common portal.
* Confirm whether the existing GSTIN is categorized as a *Taxpayer* or *Tax Deductor*.
* Never use a regular supplier GSTIN to file Form GSTR-7.
* Consult the Sikkim Commercial Taxes Division before applying, to prevent duplicate or conflicting profiles.

## 19. Administrative Handover Protocol for Outgoing DDOs

Because the GSTIN belongs to the Government establishment rather than the individual officer, transfers require a formal administrative handover.

When a DDO is transferred, promoted or superannuated:

1. Prepare a formal handover note documenting the deductor GSTIN and portal credentials.
2. Transfer custody of the registration dossier and physical registers.
3. List all pending Form GSTR-7 returns and unremitted tax withholdings.
4. Update the primary authorised signatory and DDO details on the GST portal via a non-core amendment.
5. Update the registered mobile number and institutional email address.
6. Complete and execute a formal compliance handover memorandum.

### Handover Checklist

* [ ] Form GST REG-06 original certificate transferred.
* [ ] ARN acknowledgement and registration dossiers transferred.
* [ ] GST portal user ID and password transferred securely.
* [ ] Registered mobile and institutional email verified and updated.
* [ ] Schedule of pending monthly returns (Form GSTR-7) verified.
* [ ] Schedule of unremitted tax deductions reconciled.
* [ ] Current GST-TDS register verified and signed by both officers.
* [ ] Form GSTR-7A certificate distribution log verified.
* [ ] Pending audit paras or clarification notices documented.
* [ ] Joint handover memorandum signed and submitted to the Head of Office.

## 20. Office Reorganisation, Mergers and Cancellation

If a Government office is closed, merged into another administrative unit, or ceases to disburse taxable contracts exceeding statutory thresholds:

1. Verify that all outstanding tax deductions have been remitted to the Government account.
2. Ensure all pending monthly returns in Form GSTR-7 are filed up to date.
3. Apply for cancellation of registration using Form GST REG-07 on the GST common portal.
4. The proper officer will verify the application and issue a formal cancellation order in Form GST REG-08 pursuant to Rule 12 of the CGST Rules, 2017.
5. Retain all historical accounting books, registers, vouchers and returns for at least 72 months from the annual return due date, in accordance with Section 36 of the CGST Act.

## 21. Common Registration Deficiencies

* **Name discrepancies:** Entering an informal department title that fails automated verification against Income Tax PAN or TAN records.
* **Incorrect jurisdictional selection:** Choosing an incorrect ward, circle or Central Range in Sikkim, which delays processing.
* **Using personal contact credentials:** Registering personal mobile numbers and email addresses that become inaccessible when an officer transfers out.
* **Omission of formal authorisation:** Uploading internal notes instead of a formal Government office order authorising the signatory.
* **Incorrect taxpayer constitution:** Selecting regular *Taxpayer* instead of *Tax Deductor* in Part A.
* **Conflating identifiers:** Attempting to use a contractor's GSTIN or substituting a departmental PAN for a TAN.
* **Unmanaged transfers:** Failing to update the authorised signatory on the portal after a change in DDO personnel.

## 22. Four-Tier Internal Control Structure

To maintain compliance and prepare for audit reviews, implement a four-tier internal control system:

```text
Level 1: Bill Scrutiny Section
(Verifies supplier status, taxable contract value > ₹2,50,000, and invoice accuracy)
                          ↓
Level 2: Accounts Verification Section
(Computes 2% TDS, validates deductor GSTIN, and posts register entries)
                          ↓
Level 3: DDO Payment Sanction
(Verifies budget authority, signs payment voucher, and passes net amount)
                          ↓
Level 4: Tax Compliance & Reconciliation Unit
(Deposits deductions by the 10th, files Form GSTR-7, and tracks Form GSTR-7A)

```

## 25. Core Operational Takeaways

1. **Institutional ownership:** GST-TDS registration belongs to the Government office or local body, not to the DDO personally.
2. **Dedicated application:** Deductor registration is initiated exclusively via Form GST REG-07 under the *Tax Deductor* category.
3. **Identifier integrity:** Never confuse the deductor GSTIN with the supplier's GSTIN or an Income Tax TAN.
4. **Mandatory threshold:** Deduct 2% GST-TDS (1% CGST + 1% SGST or 2% IGST) on taxable supplies where the individual contract value exceeds ₹2,50,000 (excluding taxes).
5. **Strict deadlines:** Remit deducted taxes and submit Form GSTR-7 returns on or before the 10th day of the following calendar month.
6. **Audit trail:** Maintain the Form GST REG-06 certificate, monthly tax registers, challans, Form GSTR-7 receipts and Form GSTR-7A certificates for at least 72 months from the annual return due date.
